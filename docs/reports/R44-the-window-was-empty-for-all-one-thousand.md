## 1. 증상 / Symptom

Step 4 of the documented recommendation rule — *filtered to a difficulty band matched to their
recent accuracy* — was not implemented. The band was `3..7` for every learner and every request
this repository has ever served.

The reason recorded beside it was the cost of a second pass over `attempt` on a schema with no
index for it. That index has existed since `V2` on 2026-08-12 (`R43`).

So the deviation outlived its reason. **The question is what was actually blocking it, and the
answer is that nobody had said what *recent* means.** Two readings are in ordinary use:

| | fixes | lets vary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| last `N` **attempts** | the sample size | the period it spans |
| last `D` **days** | the period | the sample size |

The usual argument for caring is a population with different cadences. **This report does not
assume that population exists here. It measures whether it does**, and it does not.

## 2. 재현 / Reproduction

```bash
./gradlew :seed:test --tests '*RecencyDefinitionTest*' --rerun-tasks
```

The test prints the block quoted in §3 to standard out and asserts two of its rows. No database,
no container, no network.

### 3.1 The premise the trap rests on is not in this dataset

**Spread zero.** Every one of the thousand learners made exactly **39** attempts in the last
seven days, and the last twenty attempts span 81–83 hours for all of them. There is no learner
here doing a hundred a day and none doing three a week.

`Generator.writeAttempts` is why, in one line:

```kotlin
val at = start + (windowSeconds * n) / scale.attemptsPerLearner + r.nextInt(3600)
```

`n` is the per-learner attempt index and `scale.attemptsPerLearner` is a constant. **There is no
learner-dependent term.** Every learner is on the same 4.32-hour cadence, ±1 hour of jitter, and
the two definitions are therefore a constant apart for all of them. The 2-hour span spread and
the 0 count spread are that jitter and nothing else.

### 3.2 They disagree anyway, and not for the reason the choice is argued from

**158 of 1,000 — 15.8%** get a different difficulty band depending on which definition is used,
on a population with **no cadence variation at all.**

The mechanism is sample size alone. *Last 20 attempts* draws 20; *last 7 days* draws 39. Two
samples of different size from the same stream give different accuracies, and often enough they
land on opposite sides of a threshold. **One learner in six is banded differently by a choice
that the textbook argument says should not matter for this population.**

That is the headline, and it is not the headline the trap predicted. The trap predicted
divergence from heterogeneous cadence. **The cadence is uniform and the divergence is 15.8%.**

### 3.3 Against the clock the application actually uses, step 4 does nothing

The dataset's newest attempt is `2026-08-09T20:40:41Z`. `Generator.BASE_INSTANT` is fixed at
`2026-08-10T00:00:00Z` on purpose — *"Fixed, so the dataset does not shift with the calendar"* —
so the gap to the wall clock grows by one day per day.

Measured at `2026-08-22T03:45:20Z`, the gap is **12.3 days** and `RECENT_DAYS` is 7. So:

> **1,000 of 1,000 learners have an empty window, every band falls back to `NO_EVIDENCE = 3..7`,
> and that is the exact constant step 4 was implemented to replace.**

Implementing step 4 changes **no recommendation for any learner** on the shipped dataset. Every
unit test written against fresh data passes. Every integration test that inserts an attempt and
reads it back passes. The feature is present, correct, covered, and inert.

### 3.5 The obvious shape of step 4 is `R1`'s defect, and the gate caught it

The first working version of `RecommendationService` read:

```kotlin
@Transactional(readOnly = true) fun nextRows(...)          { … difficultyBandFor(learnerId) … }
@Transactional(readOnly = true) fun difficultyBandFor(...) = bandFor(evidenceFor(learnerId, basis))
@Transactional(readOnly = true) fun evidenceFor(...)       = …
```

`TransactionBoundaryRulesTest` refused it, verbatim:

```
Rule 'methods that are annotated with @Transactional should not be called from within their own
class, because a call through `this` does not reach the Spring proxy, so @Transactional on the
target does nothing -- observed at 21e7162, fixed at 9388743' was violated (1 times):
  RecommendationService.evidenceFor(...) is called from RecommendationService.difficultyBandFor(...)
```

**That is `R1`, reintroduced by the obvious implementation of step 4, and caught by `R1`'s own
gate on the first run.** The remedy is one private unannotated method that all three public
entry points call; a private method has no proxy to miss.

**It was violated *once*, and it should have been twice.** `nextRows` → `difficultyBandFor` is
the same defect and went unreported. `R45` is that finding: the exemption `R7` §3.5 added to stop
the rule flagging Kotlin's `$default` bridges was also hiding real self-invocations behind them.

## 4. 원인 / Mechanism

Two mechanisms, and they are unrelated to each other.

**§3.2 — different sample sizes are different estimators.** Accuracy over 20 Bernoulli draws and
accuracy over 39 from the same underlying rate differ by sampling noise. With three thresholds
across `0..1`, a difference of one or two correct answers moves a learner across a boundary. The
15.8% is the rate at which that happens here. It is a property of *thresholding a noisy estimate*
and it would exist for any two window sizes, which is why it survives a population with no
cadence variation.

**§3.3 — a fixed dataset and a moving clock.** The seed pins its newest attempt so that numbers
taken on it stay comparable. The application asks *what happened in the last seven days* of the
real clock. Both are correct in isolation. Together they guarantee that the answer becomes
*nothing* on a known date and stays that way, and **no test relates them**, because a test that
inserts its own attempt supplies its own recency and never consults the seed's.

## 5. 처방 / Remedy

`ADR-021` decides the definition. What it does **not** decide is §3.3, and this section is why
that is a judgement rather than an errand.

| Option for §3.3 | Effect | Cost | Chosen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leave it | Step 4 is inert on the shipped seed and nothing says so at run time | zero | **no** |
| Move `BASE_INSTANT` to generation time | Windows always populated | **Every published number in this repository is on a fixed dataset.** A seed that shifts with the calendar makes `R3`, `R20`, `R28` and every plan comparison non-reproducible. `Scale`'s KDoc already refuses this class of change | no |
| Compute the window from the data's own maximum | Step 4 works on the seed | The application would be asking *recent relative to the newest row*, which is not what step 4 means and is wrong the moment there is real traffic | no |
| **Record it, and make the inertness visible rather than silent** | Nothing is hidden. The band still says `NO_EVIDENCE`, which is true | does not fix it | **yes, for now — and it is `open.md`'s, not this report's** |

The fourth option is what `RecentAccuracy.Evidence` is for: `attempts` travels with the band, so
a caller holding a `3..7` can tell *no evidence* from *evidence that says 3..7*. **That is not a
fix. It is the difference between an inert feature and an inert feature that admits it.**

## 7. 회귀 게이트 / Regression gate

`seed/src/test/kotlin/net/gseek/proxima/seed/RecencyDefinitionTest.kt`, two assertions, and
**neither is a threshold that was tuned to pass:**

- the last-20 span must not vary by more than 2 h across learners. If it does, the generator has
  gained a per-learner cadence and §3.1's conclusion is void — which is the only reason this is
  an assertion rather than a printed number.
- no learner may have an empty window measured against **the dataset's own** newest attempt. This
  does **not** guard §3.3, and cannot: §3.3 is about the wall clock, and an assertion about the
  wall clock would fail on a fixed date for reasons no commit caused.

`QueryCountTest` gates §3.4 exactly, in both directions.

**Nothing gates §3.3.** That is stated here rather than implied, and it is §8's first bullet.

## 8. 남는 위험 / Remaining risk

- **The defect in §3.3 has no gate and no fix.** Step 4 is inert on the shipped seed and nothing
  fails. A gate would have to compare the seed's fixed `BASE_INSTANT` against the application's
  window, and it would go red on a date rather than on a commit — which `R27` §5 already argued
  against and `ADR-017` only accepted after the pin made it start green. There is no equivalent
  here. **This needs a judgement and belongs in `docs/decisions/open.md`, not in this bullet.**
- **A second window is on the same clock and has not expired yet.** Step 3 uses
  `RECENCY_WINDOW = 30 days`. The newest attempt is 2026-08-09, so **from about 2026-09-09 the
  `NOT EXISTS` will exclude nothing for anybody** and the rule will start recommending items every
  learner has already attempted. **미측정** — no test asserts it, none is added here, and the date
  is arithmetic on a measured maximum rather than an observation.
- **Not one number in this report went through PostgreSQL.** The 15.8% is computed over the
  generator's output, not over the loaded database. It should be identical — the loader `COPY`s
  those exact bytes — but *should be* is not *measured*, and `R9` is the report about what an
  untested equivalence is worth. **미측정: the same comparison against the loaded 3,000,000 rows.**
- **미측정: the plan cost of the tie-break.** `recentOutcomesByCount` orders by
  `attempted_at desc, id desc`. Whether `V2`'s index still serves that without a sort node was
  **not measured**, and the slice brief required H2's performance to be measured under `R3`'s
  conditions or the comparison refused. **The comparison is refused.** No latency figure for
  step 4 appears anywhere in this report.
- **The band thresholds exist in two modules with nothing relating them.** `RecentAccuracy` holds
  them in `api`; `RecencyDefinitionTest` restates them in `seed`, which cannot depend on `api`.
  This is `ADR-006`'s shape **without `ADR-006`'s gate** — `ADR-006` earned its duplication by
  adding `ScoreBandGateTest`. Nothing here fails if the two drift apart. That is a gap, not a
  trade.
- **`LAST_N_DAYS` can band a learner off three attempts and this is not prevented.** `ADR-021`
  records it as the chosen option's real defect. A minimum-sample rule was **not** written because
  this seed offers no data to choose a threshold from — every learner has 39.
- **15.8% is the number for a population with no cadence variation, which is the opposite of the
  case the choice is usually argued about.** **미측정: what it becomes when learners actually
  differ.** Nothing in this repository can produce that number without a different generator, and
  changing the generator changes every other published figure.
- **What would break this conclusion.** Any change to `Generator.writeAttempts` that introduces a
  learner-dependent term in the timestamp. §7's first assertion is there to catch exactly that,
  and it is the only thing standing between this report and silently becoming false.

## 9. 배운 것 / What I learned

이 트랩은 "학습자마다 리듬이 다르면 두 정의가 갈린다"를 보여달라는 것이었고, 저는 그걸
보여줄 수 있을 거라고 생각하면서 계측기를 만들었습니다. 결과는 **spread 0**이었습니다.
천 명 전원이 최근 7일에 정확히 39개씩 풀었습니다. 시드에는 그 인구가 아예 없습니다.

여기서 멈췄으면 "재현 안 됨"으로 끝났을 겁니다. 그런데 밴드는 **15.8%가 갈렸습니다.**
리듬이 완전히 균일한데도요. 이유는 표본 크기였습니다 — 20개와 39개는 같은 스트림에서 뽑아도
다른 추정치고, 임계값이 셋이면 여섯 명 중 한 명이 다른 칸에 떨어집니다. **전제는 틀렸는데
숫자는 나왔고, 메커니즘이 예상과 달랐습니다.** 이게 제일 배운 것입니다. 트랩이 예측한 이유로
숫자가 나왔는지 확인하지 않았으면 저는 틀린 설명을 붙인 맞는 숫자를 발표했을 겁니다.

제일 아픈 건 세 번째입니다. 구현을 끝내고 나서 벽시계 기준으로 재보니 **천 명 전원의 창이
비어 있었습니다.** 시드의 마지막 시도가 12.3일 전이고 창은 7일이니까요. 그래서 제가 구현한
step 4는 그것이 대체하려던 상수 `3..7`을 전원에게 그대로 돌려주고 있습니다. 기능은 있고,
맞고, 테스트도 통과하고, **아무 일도 하지 않습니다.** 그리고 이걸 알아챈 건 계측을 한 번 더
했기 때문이지 코드를 봐서가 아닙니다. 구현이 끝났다고 생각한 지점에서 한 번 더 재는 것 —
그게 이 저장소가 계속 가르치는 것인데 저는 매번 새로 배웁니다.

마지막으로 하나 인정할 것: 이 리포트의 숫자는 **데이터베이스를 한 번도 거치지 않았습니다.**
생성기 출력 위에서 계산했습니다. 같아야 하지만 같은지는 재지 않았고, §8에 미측정으로
적었습니다. 시간이 없어서 그런 거고, 그게 이유가 되지 않는다는 것도 압니다.
