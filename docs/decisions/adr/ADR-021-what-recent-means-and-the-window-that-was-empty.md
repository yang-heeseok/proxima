# ADR-021 — *Recent* is a span of days, and on the shipped seed that window is empty for everybody

> **Created**: 2026-08-22
> **Updated**: 2026-08-22
> **Status**: Accepted

## Context

Step 4 of the recommendation rule in `domain-model.md` reads *filtered to a difficulty band
matched to their recent accuracy*. It was not implemented. The band was the constant `3..7`,
and the KDoc beside it gave the reason as a second pass over three million `attempt` rows on a
schema with no index for it.

**That reason had been false for ten days.** `V2` created `(learner_id, attempted_at)` on
2026-08-12 and `R3` measured the read at 0.056 ms against 36.6 ms without it. `R43` is the
report about the sentence outliving the fact, and about the gate that now reads code comments.

So the index was never what was missing. **What was missing was a decision**, and it is the one
below.

Two readings of *recent* are in ordinary use:

| | fixes | lets vary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| last `N` **attempts** | the sample size | the period it spans |
| last `D` **days** | the period | the sample size |

## What was measured before choosing

`RecencyDefinitionTest`, against `Scale.FULL` — 1,000 learners × 3,000 attempts = 3,000,000
rows at seed value 20260810, generated by the shipped `Generator` rather than by a copy of its
arithmetic. `R44` §3 carries the run.

| | |
| --- | ---: |
| span of the last 20 attempts — min / max across 1,000 learners | **81 h / 83 h** |
| attempts in the last 7 days — min / max across 1,000 learners | **39 / 39** |
| learners whose band **disagrees** between the two definitions | **158 of 1,000 — 15.8%** |

**The first two rows say the argument for choosing does not apply to this dataset, and the
third says the choice matters anyway.** `Generator.writeAttempts` spaces every learner's
attempts by a constant with no learner-dependent term, so there is no learner here doing a
hundred a day and none doing three a week — the 7-day count is **39 for every single learner,
spread zero.** The population the textbook argument is about does not exist in this seed.

The bands still disagree for 15.8%, and **the mechanism is not the one the choice is usually
argued from.** It is sample size alone: 20 attempts against 39 attempts, drawn from the same
stream, land on opposite sides of a threshold often enough to move one learner in six.

## Decision

**`Basis.LAST_N_DAYS`, `D = 7`. When the window holds nothing, the band is `NO_EVIDENCE = 3..7`
and says so, rather than being computed from whatever few rows exist.**

**Why days and not attempts — one argument, and it is about staleness rather than statistics.**
*Last 20 attempts* has no notion of time at all. A learner who stopped six months ago has their
six-month-old accuracy treated as current, forever, and the band never changes because the
sample never changes. Learning is the thing this domain is about; a definition of *recent* that
a year cannot move is not a definition of recent.

**What the rejected reading did better, which is the half usually lost.** *Last N attempts*
**guarantees the denominator.** It cannot produce a band computed from three attempts, and
`LAST_N_DAYS` can and will for any barely-active learner. That is a real defect in the chosen
option, it is not fixed here, and it is why `RecentAccuracy.Evidence` carries `attempts`
alongside the band: **the sample size travels with the answer**, so a caller can see a band that
came off nothing. A minimum-sample rule was considered and **not** written, because there is no
data here to choose the threshold from — every learner in this seed has 39 — and a constant
picked without a measurement is the thing this repository refuses.

## What this decision produced, which is the reason to read it

Implementing step 4 did not fix anything. It produced a defect, and the defect is the best thing
in this round:

> **On the shipped dataset the implemented step 4 returns, for all 1,000 learners, the exact
> constant it was written to replace.** The feature is present, correct, covered by tests, and
> inert. Every unit test written against fresh data passes. **That is the defect class this
> repository exists to collect, and here it arrived inside the fix for something else.**

The measurement is below. It was found by re-measuring at the point the work looked finished,
not by reading the code.

## Consequences

### On the shipped seed, step 4 is inert, and this is measured rather than predicted

`RecommendationService` computes the window as `Instant.now(clock).minus(RECENT_DAYS)`. The seed
does not move with the calendar — `Generator.BASE_INSTANT` is fixed at `2026-08-10T00:00:00Z`,
deliberately, so that the dataset does not shift under the numbers taken on it. Measured at
`2026-08-22T03:45:20Z`:

| | |
| --- | ---: |
| newest attempt in the dataset | `2026-08-09T20:40:41Z` |
| dataset older than the clock by | **12.3 days** |
| learners with an empty 7-day window, measured from now | **1,000 of 1,000** |

**So every band falls back to `3..7` — the exact constant step 4 replaced.** Implementing step 4
against this dataset changes no recommendation for any learner, and **every unit test written
with fresh data passes.** That is the defect class this repository exists to collect, and here
it arrived inside the fix for something else.

This is not a reason to reject the decision. It is a reason the decision cannot be validated on
the shipped seed, and `R44` §8 says so rather than implying coverage.

### A second window is on the same clock and has not expired yet

Step 3 — *items this learner has not attempted in 30 days* — uses `RECENCY_WINDOW = 30 days`
against the same fixed dataset. The newest attempt is `2026-08-09`. **From about 2026-09-09 the
`NOT EXISTS` in `findRecommendations` will exclude nothing for anybody**, because every attempt
will be older than the window, and the rule will silently start recommending items every learner
has already seen. Nothing in the tree fails on that day. It is written here because it is the
same mechanism one step up, and it is 미측정 — no test asserts it and none is added by this ADR.

### The band moves as the window slides, and that is a specification rather than a defect

`R44` §3.6 measures it: **25 of 1,000 learners (2.5%)** are one boundary crossing away from a
different band, and a crossing arrives for each learner every 4.32 hours on this dataset.

**Recorded here as a decision because `R44` asked for one.** A band computed from recent work is
*supposed* to change as that work ages out. A band that never moved would be exactly
`LAST_N_ATTEMPTS`'s defect — the reading this ADR rejected because a year cannot move it. What
would turn the wobble into a defect is a caller that treats the band as stable, and none does.

**A midnight-anchored window would remove the wobble and was not chosen**, for two reasons that
are worse than the wobble: every learner's band would change at the **same instant** rather than
spreading across the day, converting a diffuse 2.5% into one synchronised recomputation for the
whole population; and it would reintroduce *whose* midnight, which a rolling window does not have
to answer at all.

### It costs one statement

`QueryCountTest` is re-baselined in the commit that implements this: `nextRows` **1 → 2**,
`nextItems` **2 → 3**, and the association-reading path **2 + n → 3 + n**. The assertions stay
exact — a third statement on `nextRows` still fails — so this is a re-baseline against a
deliberate change, not a loosened threshold. `BaselineMigrationTest` records the same process for
`V2`'s index.

### The thresholds exist in two modules and nothing yet relates them

`RecentAccuracy.bandFor` holds them in `api`; `RecencyDefinitionTest` restates them in `seed`,
which cannot depend on `api`. This is `ADR-006`'s shape — two definitions of one rule — **without
`ADR-006`'s gate.** `ADR-006` earned its duplication by adding `ScoreBandGateTest` to relate the
two; there is no equivalent here, and that is a gap rather than a trade. It is `R44` §8.

## What was not measured

- **Whether 7 days is the right period, or 20 the right count.** Both are `ADR-021`'s by
  assertion. The seed cannot distinguish them: it has one cadence.
- **The two definitions against a heterogeneous population.** Nothing measured here says what
  15.8% becomes when learners actually differ. It is the number for a uniform population, which
  is the opposite of the case the choice is argued about.
- **The plan cost of `recentOutcomesByCount`'s tie-break.** `order by attempted_at desc, id desc`
  may or may not still be served by `V2`'s index without a sort; **미측정**, and `R44` §8 says so.
- **Anything at all under load.** Every number here is a single-threaded computation over a
  generated file. No figure in this ADR may be placed beside a `p99`.
